SAGE

The stronghold of China, Sage creates safety for herself and her team wherever she goes. Able to revive fallen friends and stave off aggressive pushes, she provides a calm center to a hellish fight.

CYPHER

The Moroccan information broker, Cypher is a one-man surveillance network who keeps tabs on the enemy's every move. No secret is safe. No maneuver goes unseen. Cypher is always watching.

KILLJOY

The genius of Germany. Killjoy secures the battlefield with ease using her arsenal of inventions. If the damage from her gear doesn't stop her enemies, her robots' debuff will help make short work of them.

CHAMBER

Well dressed and well armed, French weapons designer Chamber expels aggressors with deadly precision. He leverages his custom arsenal to hold the line and pick off enemies from afar, with a contingency built for every plan.

DEADLOCK

Norway's Deadlock holds her ground with advanced nanowire technology. Her traps and barriers stop enemy advances, catching opponents who try to break through her watch.

VYSE

Vyse shapes liquid metal into a battlefield of traps. She separates enemies from their teammates, blinds those who approach, and disables their weapons when they need them most.

VETO

Senegal's Veto uses his powerful mutation to shut down enemy abilities and equipment. He strips away his opponents' advantages and forces them to rely on their aim.

JETT

Representing her home country of South Korea, Jett's agile and evasive fighting style lets her take risks no one else can. She runs circles around every skirmish, cutting enemies before they even know what hit them.

PHOENIX

Hailing from the U.K., Phoenix's star power shines through in his fighting style, igniting the battlefield with flash and flare. Whether he's got backup or not, he'll rush into a fight on his own terms.

NEON

Filipino Agent Neon surges forward at shocking speeds, discharging bursts of bioelectric radiance as fast as her body generates it. She races ahead to catch enemies off guard, then strikes them down quicker than lightning.

REYNA

Forged in the heart of Mexico, Reyna dominates single combat, popping off with each kill she scores. Her capability is only limited by her raw skill, making her highly dependent on performance.

YORU

Japanese native, Yoru, rips holes straight through reality to infiltrate enemy lines unseen. Using deception and aggression in equal measure, he gets the drop on each target before they know where to look.

RAZE

Brazil's Raze brings explosive firepower to every fight. With grenades, a hunting robot, and her rocket launcher, she drives enemies out of cover and blasts open a path for her team.

ISO

Iso, a hired gun from China, turns energy into protection as he pushes forward. His focus and shielding help him challenge opponents and take control of one-on-one fights.

WAYLAY

Thailand's Waylay becomes light to race into enemy territory. She slows opponents with prismatic energy, attacks from unexpected angles, and returns to safety before they can respond.

Sova

Born from the eternal winter of Russia's tundra, Sova tracks, finds, and eliminates enemies with ruthless efficiency and precision. His custom bow and incredible scouting abilities ensure that even if you run, you cannot hide.

Kayo

KAY/O is a machine of war built for a single purpose: neutralizing radiants. His power to suppress enemy abilities cripples his opponents' capacity to fight back, securing him and his allies the ultimate edge.

Fade

Turkish bounty hunter, Fade, unleashes the power of raw nightmares to seize enemy secrets. Attuned with terror itself, she hunts targets and reveals their deepest fears—before crushing them in the dark.

Breach

Breach, the bionic Swede, fires powerful, targeted kinetic blasts to aggressively clear a path through enemy ground. The damage and disruption he inflicts ensures no fight is ever fair.

Skye

Australia's Skye guides her animal creations into danger to help her team advance. She scouts ahead, disrupts opponents, and heals nearby allies so they can keep fighting together.

Gekko

Gekko comes from Los Angeles with a lively crew of creatures. They blind, stun, and chase opponents, then return to him so he can gather them up for another attack.

Tejo

Colombian intelligence veteran Tejo uses guided explosives to force enemies out of position. His drone uncovers threats while carefully placed strikes clear the way for his teammates.

Viper

The American chemist, Viper deploys an array of poisonous chemical devices to control the battlefield and cripple the enemy's vision. If the toxins don't kill her prey, her mind games surely will.

Omen

A phantom of a memory, Omen hunts in the shadows. He renders enemies blind, teleports across the field, then lets paranoia take hold as his foe scrambles to learn where he might strike next.

Harbour

Hailing from India’s coast, Harbor storms the field wielding ancient technology with dominion over water. He unleashes frothing rapids and crushing waves to shield his allies and pummel those that oppose him.

Clove

Scottish immortal Clove clouds the battlefield and weakens opponents with their unusual powers. Even after falling in combat, they can place smokes and find a way back into the fight.

Miks

Croatia's Miks channels sound into powers that bring his team together. He blocks sightlines, supports allies, and sends sonic pulses across the battlefield to shape the pace of a fight.
